# PAGENO — DESIGN.md

## Core concept
> Your money, one page at a time.

PAGENO turns personal finance into a book: your financial life is a book you keep updating.

## Product vocabulary
| Finance | PAGENO |
|---|---|
| Transaction | Entry |
| Month | Page |
| Year | Volume |
| Savings goal | Bookmark |
| Category/account | Section |
| Monthly summary | Page summary |
| History | Previous pages |

## Visual system
Primary: #2E2723, #F4A259, #F7F4ED, #EADCC8.
Semantic: #6F8F72 income/positive, #C96A4A expense/negative.
Supporting: #858078 secondary text, #FBFAF7 background.

## Logo
Lowercase `pageno`, rounded geometry, friendly book mascot, orange bookmark accent. Secondary marks: mascot alone and simple face/mark for app icon.

## Illustration
Rounded geometry, paper/book shapes, tiny expressive faces, subtle orange motion marks, generous whitespace. Avoid realistic money, piles of coins, bank buildings, stock-chart decoration, generic fintech gradients.

## Production note
The generated board is a visual direction, not final vector artwork. Recreate selected marks in Figma/Illustrator/SVG before shipping.
